# Legal Rights Awareness Assistant

### Understand Your Rights. Know the Law. Take Action.

An AI-powered legal awareness and assistance platform designed to make Indian laws accessible, understandable, and actionable for everyone.

Legal Rights Awareness Assistant bridges the gap between complex legal systems and ordinary citizens by combining multilingual AI, verified legal information, document intelligence, judicial research, and legal aid discovery.

The platform enables users to ask legal questions in their own language, understand their rights, simplify legal documents, explore relevant judgments, and discover available legal assistance.

## About the Project

Access to justice begins with awareness.

Millions of people encounter legal problems involving employment, housing, consumer protection, family matters, cybercrime, property disputes, and other everyday situations. However, understanding which laws apply and where to seek assistance can be difficult.

Legal information is often distributed across government portals, legal documents, court judgments, and complicated legislation. Language barriers and limited awareness make this information even harder to access.

**Legal Rights Awareness Assistant** aims to make legal knowledge accessible through an intelligent, multilingual platform that explains complex legal information in simple language while maintaining traceability to authoritative sources.

The goal is not simply to build another AI chatbot. It is to create a reliable legal information and assistance ecosystem.

## Problem Statement

People frequently face the following challenges:

- Limited awareness of their fundamental and legal rights.
- Difficulty understanding complex legal terminology.
- Language barriers when accessing legal information.
- Lack of awareness about applicable laws and legal provisions.
- Difficulty finding relevant court judgments.
- Limited access to affordable legal assistance.
- Difficulty understanding legal notices, agreements, and official documents.
- Misinformation and unreliable legal advice from unverified online sources.
- Lack of clarity about the next steps when facing a legal problem.

## Our Solution

We are building a centralized platform that combines AI-powered legal assistance with verified legal information and access to government-supported legal services.

Users can:

1. Describe a legal problem in their preferred language.
2. Receive an explanation of potentially applicable legal rights.
3. Understand relevant laws and legal provisions in simple language.
4. Upload legal documents for AI-assisted analysis.
5. Explore relevant judicial decisions and case studies.
6. Find nearby legal aid authorities.
7. Access official emergency and assistance contacts.
8. Save legal issues and receive optional follow-up reminders.

The assistant aims to explain legal information in a way that ordinary citizens can understand without requiring prior legal knowledge.

## 2. Multimodal Legal Understanding

Legal problems are not always communicated through text.

The platform supports multiple input methods.

**Text-to-Text**

- Receive answers in the preferred language.
- Simplify complex legal terminology.
- Translate explanations while preserving legal meaning.

**Image-to-Text**

- Extract text using OCR.
- Identify important legal information.
- Detect relevant dates, names, clauses, and references.
- Generate simplified explanations.

**Document Intelligence**
- PDF document processing.
- Legal notice analysis.
- Agreement and contract understanding.
- Clause extraction.
- Potential risk identification.
- Legal provision identification.
- Document comparison.

- Case citation verification.

## 8. AI Case Follow-up System

Legal problems often require multiple steps rather than a single interaction.

The platform supports optional follow-ups to help users track progress.

**Workflow:**

1. User discusses a legal problem.
2. AI explains relevant rights and possible next steps.
3. User optionally schedules a seven-day follow-up.
4. The system sends a reminder with consent.
5. AI asks about progress and new developments.
6. Recommendations are reassessed using updated legal information.

Users remain in control of their saved cases and notifications.

## Source-Grounded Legal Retrieval

The legal intelligence pipeline follows a structured process.

```text
USER INPUT
    |
    v
INPUT PROCESSING
    |
    v
LANGUAGE DETECTION
    |
    v
LEGAL INTENT CLASSIFICATION
    |
    v
JURISDICTION DETECTION
    |
    v
LEGAL INFORMATION RETRIEVAL
    |
    v
OFFICIAL SOURCE VERIFICATION
    |
    v
JUDGMENT RETRIEVAL
    |
    v
LEGAL HIERARCHY AND CONFLICT ANALYSIS
    |
    v
EVIDENCE EVALUATION
    |
    v
AI RESPONSE GENERATION
    |
    v
CITATION VALIDATION
    |
    v
VERIFIED LEGAL EXPLANATION
```

## Hallucination Prevention

The platform is designed to minimize fabricated legal information through multiple validation mechanisms.

- Verify whether cited legislation actually exists.
- Validate section numbers against original legal text.
- Check amendment and commencement dates.
- Verify judgment references against official documents.
- Identify outdated or repealed provisions.
- Check territorial and jurisdictional applicability.
- Evaluate evidence coverage before generating conclusions.
- Abstain when reliable supporting evidence is unavailable.

A model-generated confidence score alone is never treated as proof of legal correctness.

## Legal Hierarchy and Conflict Resolution

Legal interpretation must account for:

- Constitutional provisions.
- Central legislation.
- State legislation.
- Rules and regulations.
- Official notifications.
- Binding judicial precedents.
- Persuasive judicial decisions.
- Amendments and repeals.
- Commencement dates.
- Territorial applicability.

The system must account for constitutional supremacy, legislative competence, judicial precedent, and changes in applicable law rather than relying on a simplistic fixed ranking.

## Why Ollama?

[Ollama](https://ollama.com/) provides a convenient way to run large language models locally.

It is used as the local inference runtime for compatible models.

Benefits include:

- Local model execution.
- Model flexibility.
- Reduced dependency on hosted inference services.
- Local API access.
- Support for different model sizes.
- Greater control over inference infrastructure.

**Important:** Ollama is the model runtime, not the legal knowledge database. Legal retrieval, source verification, and citation validation are separate application responsibilities.

# Getting Started

Follow these instructions to configure the development environment.

## Prerequisites

Make sure the following tools are installed:

- Git
- Node.js
- npm
- Python 3.11 or later
- Ollama
- PostgreSQL
- Redis (if required by the configured services)

## 1. Clone the Repository

```bash
git clone YOUR_REPOSITORY_URL
cd YOUR_PROJECT_DIRECTORY
```

## 2. Install Frontend Dependencies

```bash
npm install
```

## 3. Install Ollama

Download Ollama for your operating system:

https://ollama.com/download

Verify the installation:

```bash
ollama --version
```

## 4. Download a Language Model

Select a model compatible with your hardware and application requirements.

For example:

```bash
ollama pull qwen2.5:3b
```

Run the model:

```bash
ollama run qwen2.5:3b
```

Check the local API:

```text
http://localhost:11434
```

The model shown above is an example. Actual model selection should be based on multilingual performance, legal reasoning evaluation, hardware requirements, and the model's license.

## 5. Configure the Backend

```bash
cd backend

python -m venv venv

# macOS / Linux
source venv/bin/activate

pip install -r requirements.txt
```

Start the backend using the actual application entry point:

```bash
uvicorn main:app --reload --port 8000
```

## 6. Configure Environment Variables

Create the required environment files using the project's environment configuration.

Example:

```env
# Application
APP_NAME=Legal Rights Awareness Assistant
APP_ENV=development

# Frontend
NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URL=http://localhost:3000

# Backend
BACKEND_URL=http://localhost:8000

# Ollama
OLLAMA_BASE_URL=http://localhost:11434
OLLAMA_MODEL=qwen2.5:3b

# Database
DATABASE_URL=postgresql://USER:PASSWORD@localhost:5432/legal_assistant

# Redis
REDIS_URL=redis://localhost:6379

# Authentication
JWT_SECRET=CHANGE_THIS_SECRET
```

Use the actual variable names defined in the repository. Never commit real credentials or secrets.

## 7. Start the Frontend

From the frontend project directory:

```bash
npm run dev
```

Open the application:

http://localhost:3000

# Security and Privacy

Legal documents and personal legal situations can contain sensitive information.

The platform aims to follow privacy-conscious engineering practices.

- Protect user documents from unauthorized access.
- Use secure authentication and session management.
- Apply access controls to stored documents.
- Avoid exposing sensitive information in logs.
- Validate external documents before processing.
- Treat retrieved webpages and document contents as untrusted input.
- Protect internal system instructions and credentials.
- Provide appropriate data retention and deletion controls.

Security measures should be enforced at the backend and infrastructure levels, not solely through AI prompts.

# Responsible AI

The platform is designed around accuracy, transparency, and responsible legal information delivery.

Its core principles are:

**Source Transparency:** Legal explanations should link to identifiable sources.

**Accuracy:** Legal provisions and judgments should be verified before being presented as verified.

**Jurisdiction Awareness:** Applicable laws may differ depending on the state, territory, court, and circumstances.

**Language Accessibility:** Legal information should be understandable in the user's preferred language.

**Uncertainty Disclosure:** The system should identify missing information and conflicting evidence.

**Responsible Abstention:** When sufficient evidence is unavailable, the system should clearly communicate its limitations instead of inventing an answer.
