# Product Vision

## Direction

Become the place a person goes to learn "what are my rights here, and what do I do next", in their own language, with every statement traceable to an official source.

Long term:

1. Many jurisdictions, each with a transparent coverage page showing exactly which sources are ingested and when they were last verified.
2. Document intelligence that explains clauses, flags risks and compares versions.
3. Action blueprints that turn a situation into ordered steps, deadlines, and the right authority to contact.
4. Offline-friendly, low-bandwidth, accessible interfaces for people with limited access.

## Product principles

1. **Evidence before eloquence.** An answer without a verifiable source is not shipped.
2. **Refuse well.** Out-of-coverage and low-confidence cases get a clear, helpful decline with a pointer to official help.
3. **Plain language.** Reading level is a feature. Legal terms are explained on first use.
4. **Jurisdiction first.** No answer is produced before the jurisdiction is known or confirmed.
5. **Privacy by default.** Collect the least. Uploaded documents are sensitive and short-lived.
6. **Honest about the prototype.** Sample content is labelled until real sources replace it.
7. **Accessible to everyone.** WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard-first, reduced-motion safe, screen-reader tested.
8. **Calm design.** Motion and polish support comprehension. They never hide or delay content.

## What success looks like

- Citation validity: 100% of shown citations resolve to a stored source (hard gate, not a target).
- Groundedness: answers contain no claim unsupported by retrieved text (measured on an evaluation set).
- A user can reach a sourced answer in under three interactions.
- Every supported jurisdiction publishes its coverage and last-verified date.

## Anti-goals

- Maximising engagement or chat length.
- Sounding authoritative when the evidence is thin.
- Hiding the source behind the answer.
